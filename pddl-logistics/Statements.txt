// Statement 1 //

It seems that FF tries to satisfy its 
goals more greedliy than IPP. FF unloads packet1
before it loads packet2 while IPP does the inverse.
Also IPP seems to treat the loading and unloading 
of packet1 at the same location as being possible
to do simultaneously while FF doesnt. It is worth to
keep in mind that IPP plans in time steps, i.e. one step
can be many actions, while one step in FF equals one action.

// Statement 2 //
The differnce between IPP and FF in this scenario is 
that IPP utilizes both Scooty-puff and truck1 to solve
the deliver the two packages in parallel. In contrast 
FF only utilizes truck1 for a sequential solution to
the delivery-problem. The differnce between the planners solution-
strategies now becomes more apparent. However the sequential solution
that FF uses in this case requires less steps to be completed than IPP.
With regards to time-steps IPP performs better.


IPP:
    time step    0: DRIVE SCOOTY-PUFF PLANEX-BUILDING OFFICE1 CITY1
                    LOAD PACKET2 TRUCK1 OFFICE2
    time step    1: DRIVE TRUCK1 OFFICE2 OFFICE1 CITY1
                    LOAD PACKET1 SCOOTY-PUFF OFFICE1
    time step    2: DRIVE SCOOTY-PUFF OFFICE1 OFFICE2 CITY1
                    UNLOAD PACKET2 TRUCK1 OFFICE1
    time step    3: UNLOAD PACKET1 SCOOTY-PUFF OFFICE2

FF:
    step    0: LOAD PACKET2 TRUCK1 OFFICE2
            1: DRIVE TRUCK1 OFFICE2 OFFICE1 CITY1
            2: LOAD PACKET1 TRUCK1 OFFICE1
            3: UNLOAD PACKET2 TRUCK1 OFFICE1
            4: DRIVE TRUCK1 OFFICE1 OFFICE2 CITY1
            5: UNLOAD PACKET1 TRUCK1 OFFICE2


// Statement 3 //

With these new constraints the differnce in optimality between the IPP planner 
and FF planner becomes more apparent. The solution brought by IPP only requires
one additional step to reach the goal state. In contrast FF now requires 2 additional
steps compared to before. It follows that utilizing parallel deliveries as the ammount
of deliveries and constraints increases, leads to a lesser ammount of necessary steps.

IPP:
    time step    0: DRIVE TRUCK1 OFFICE2 OFFICE1 CITY1
                    DRIVE SCOOTY-PUFF PLANEX-BUILDING OFFICE2 CITY1
    time step    1: LOAD_MOPED PACKET2 SCOOTY-PUFF OFFICE2
                    LOAD_TRUCK PACKET1 TRUCK1 OFFICE1
    time step    2: DRIVE TRUCK1 OFFICE1 OFFICE2 CITY1
                    DRIVE SCOOTY-PUFF OFFICE2 OFFICE1 CITY1
    time step    3: UNLOAD PACKET2 SCOOTY-PUFF OFFICE1
                    UNLOAD PACKET1 TRUCK1 OFFICE2

FF:
    step    0: DRIVE SCOOTY-PUFF PLANEX-BUILDING OFFICE2 CITY1
            1: LOAD_MOPED PACKET2 SCOOTY-PUFF OFFICE2
            2: DRIVE SCOOTY-PUFF OFFICE2 OFFICE1 CITY1
            3: UNLOAD PACKET2 SCOOTY-PUFF OFFICE1
            4: DRIVE TRUCK1 OFFICE2 OFFICE1 CITY1
            5: LOAD_TRUCK PACKET1 TRUCK1 OFFICE1
